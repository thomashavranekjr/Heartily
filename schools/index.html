The problem you already know

You already know what's broken.

You've seen it from inside the building. Guidance counselors stretched across student loads no one would design from scratch. The college-default assumption that pushes every student toward the same answer. The seniors who arrive at decision-day without a framework for the decision.

The honest gap is not effort. It is between what your guidance department is resourced to do and what every student actually needs from this moment.

Heartily was designed for this environment — not invented in spite of it.

01
Counselor load. One counselor for every 385 students, on average. The math of a 45-minute meeting and a senior cohort of 300 doesn't work, and no one needs that explained again.
02
Default-college framing. Pathways outside four-year college remain under-served by the tools you have. CTE deserves the same editorial weight as pre-med — your students rarely see it that way.
03
Engagement problem. Existing platforms get used because they're assigned. They are abandoned the day the assignment ends. You know which ones.
04
Reporting gap. "We did career exploration" is hard to say to a board when the artifact is a print-out from a personality quiz a sophomore took once.

Boundaries

What Heartily doesn't do.

Most edtech pitches don't have this section. We do — because we'd rather earn your confidence than leave you guessing.

×
Heartily does not replace guidance counselors. It extends their reach.
×
It does not tell students what career to pursue. It helps them figure it out.
×
It does not share individual student data with employers without explicit consent.
×
It does not require students to create a profile before exploring.
×
It does not contain advertising, sponsored listings, or employer-funded content dressed as guidance.
