Product Overview & Investor Brief · 2026

The state of work today

Only 21% of employees worldwide are engaged at work.
Seven in ten are going through the motions.

The disengagement crisis is not a management problem. It is a decision problem — set in motion before people ever took the job. Every disengaged forty-two-year-old was once a seventeen-year-old who made a career decision without the tools to make it well.

$8.8T Lost globally each year to disengagement
31% US engagement — lowest in a decade
1 in 5 workers trapped in "quiet cracking"

The exposure gap

Students are making life-defining decisions on almost zero information.

They know job titles. They know salary ranges. They've absorbed social expectations. None of this tells them what Tuesday afternoon actually feels like — or whether they would do it heartily.

This failure begins upstream and compounds for decades.

🔍

The Exposure Gap

Students have almost no honest access to what real careers look and feel like before committing to them. Job shadowing is ad hoc and dependent on who you know.

🪞

The Self-Knowledge Gap

Most students confuse social prestige with personal reward. They mistake tolerance for passion. Nobody helps them interrogate what they would actually do heartily.

📉

The Downstream Cost

Career regret touches a majority of professionals by midlife. The disengagement data is the long-term consequence of decisions made at seventeen without adequate support.

🚫

Nothing Addresses the Root

Engagement programs treat symptoms. Job boards facilitate transactions. Career quizzes produce personality types. Nobody is working on the upstream decision itself.

The solution

Find the work you'd
do with your whole heart.

Heartily is a career discovery and placement platform that helps students find the work they would do heartily — before they commit to a path — and then connects them to it.

01 — Discover See what Tuesday actually feels like.

Honest, unglamourized day-in-the-life content across every career. The question the content always answers: would you do this heartily?

02 — Understand Know what genuinely energizes you.

A longitudinal self-discovery engine built on behavioral signals, not a quiz. Surfaces cognitive style, reward triggers, appetite for difficulty — over weeks, not a single session.

03 — Pathfind See the honest portrait of a path.

Transparent career maps — salary trajectory, wellness data, decision gates, practitioner voices. Editorially framed. Never just a credential checklist.

The differentiator

The self-discovery engine.

Not a quiz. A conversation that develops over weeks. The platform learns from how students engage — what they return to, skip, share — not just what they explicitly answer.

💬

The editorial intervention

When a student filters only for "low stress, high salary" — Heartily pushes back. Not preachy. A specific, honest observation. The intervention is the soul of the product.

Business model

Three constituents.
One flywheel.

Students engage freely. Schools subscribe. Employers pay for a pre-qualified pipeline. Each layer makes the next more valuable.

Students & Families

Free — always

The moment Heartily costs a student anything, it ceases to be the platform it intends to be. Free access drives the scale that makes everything else valuable.

School Districts

SaaS subscription · $15–25K ACV

Career guidance tools, student interest dashboards, curriculum alignment. Annual contracts, low churn, embedded in guidance department workflow.

Employers & Sponsors

Pipeline + placement fees · $2–5K

Pre-qualified students who chose the field with open eyes. Not a cold applicant pool. For employers who understand the true cost of a bad hire.

Workforce Grants

Non-dilutive supplemental

DOL, Lumina, JPMorgan Chase workforce initiatives actively funding this category. Bridge capital and institutional credibility signal.

More students engaging deeply → richer behavioral data → better matching → better employer outcomes → more employer investment → more content → more students. ↻

Market timing

The conditions have been building for a decade. They are converging now.

The college consensus is cracking. Employer degree requirements are falling. Policy funding is expanding. And the technology to build what Heartily requires is finally mature.

01

Student Debt Crisis

The college-for-everyone consensus is cracking. ROI is questioned across all fields, not just trades.

02

Universal Skills Gap

Every sector reports credentials without practical instinct. The mismatch is structural, not generational.

03

New Collar Movement

Google, IBM, Apple have dropped degree requirements. Skills-based hiring is now mainstream.

04

Policy Momentum

DOL Apprenticeship funding and state earn-and-learn mandates are actively looking for platforms to attach to.

05

Technology Ready

Behavioral AI and marketplace matching are mature. This was not buildable at this quality five years ago.

The window is open. It will not stay open indefinitely.

First-mover advantage in the student discovery layer is meaningful if captured in the next 2–3 years.
